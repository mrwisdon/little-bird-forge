turret interior camera follows the turret - in-game verification

method
  - dev client: ./gradlew runClient. The dev run needed the LWJGL linux natives on java.library.path
    (JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS=-Djava.library.path=/tmp/mclibs) - an environment gap that predates this change
    (the 12:19 log already failed with "Failed to locate library: liblwjgl.so" on the unmodified tree).
  - windows: DISPLAY=:0 (WSLg) and DISPLAY=:99 (Xvfb 1600x1000 + openbox). :99 was used for the
    controlled captures because openbox gives the window real input focus, which first-person mouse
    look needs.
  - save: "New World" (creative, cheats). The player loads already riding the M1A5; C toggles the
    turret interior view (GroundVehicleInput.java:84-88).
  - turn input: xdotool mousemove_relative warps of 350-500 px; the driver's look lays the turret
    (MainBattleTankEntity.tickTurret:213-231), the turret slews at 3 deg/tick.
  - before/after were captured with the identical save, warp steps and capture script; the only
    difference is the six source files reverted to the backup
    docs/plan/backups/2026-09-13_pre-turret-follow/ for the "before" run.

results

  1. turret body no longer sweeps the lens (acceptance 1)
     before_settled.png / before_midslew.png: the barrel jumps between the left edge, out of frame
       (pointing at the wall, range "6 m") and the lower right across the steps; "GUN -xx deg"
       appears because the turret is still off the view axis.
     after_full_circle.png: 13 frames over a full 360 deg traverse. The barrel stays at the same
       spot in the lens (lower right, same angle) in every frame; the stone wall and terrain rotate
       a full circle behind it.
     after_settled.png / after_midslew.png: same, including captures taken 0.15 s into each step.

  2. camera yaw == turret yaw, hull turn adds correctly (acceptance 2)
     code: GroundCamera.computeCameraAngles sets the camera yaw to view.turretYawDeg(); the same
       value places the rider in MainBattleTankEntity.positionRider / InfantryFightingVehicleEntity.
     after_hull_turn.png: holding D rotates the hull while the mouse is still. The hull and world
       rotate under the lens, the barrel stays fixed in the lens - turret-stabilised, no reversal,
       no jump.
     after_rapid.png: five +/-500 px reversals. The barrel stays put; the world swings back and
       forth; no jitter and no clipping/spike visible in the frames.

  3. pitch is untouched (acceptance 4)
     only yaw is taken (followYawDeg returns the turret yaw), so the camera pitch is still
     minecraft.player.getViewXRot; the fire control pitch projection is unchanged. The GUN bearing
     readout still compares the turret with the mouse look, so it keeps reporting the slew offset
     (that is why it still flickers during a fast traverse, as before).

  residual / not measured here
     - The camera and the turret model both read the same 20 Hz synced value, so during a traverse
       the world steps at 20 Hz. No interpolation was added (the brief forbids smoothing away
       structural issues). Not judged a defect in these captures.
     - "no clipping" is judged from the captured frames only; a longer play test is still worthwhile.
     - Only the tank was exercised in game. The IFV shares the exact same code path (same
       followYawDeg call, same computeCameraAngles guard) but was not driven.
