multi resolution audit of the tank's bottom right UI (four window sizes)

metric 1: right edge margin must equal EDGE_MARGIN(6) x hudScale(2) x guiScale
metric 2: the six pixel gap between the ammo readout and the weapon bar must hold no HUD ink

  640x480  guiScale 2  bbox(376,314) 240x124  right margin 24 == 24   gap ink 560 (NOT hud ink)
  854x480  guiScale 2  bbox(590,314) 240x124  right margin 24 == 24   gap ink 0     OK
 1280x720  guiScale 3  bbox(885,471) 360x186  right margin 35 ~= 36   gap ink 0     OK
 1600x900  guiScale 3  bbox(1206,651) 360x186 right margin 34 ~= 36   gap ink 0     OK
   (the 1-2 px shortfall at scale 3 is the fill's exclusive right edge, not a layout error)

the 640x480 case, inspected by eye (item17_audit_640_overlap.png)
  the 560 "ink" pixels are the vanilla chat line and the vanilla hotbar, not the HUD. That shot does
  show one real overlap though: at this GUI width the tank's weapon bar (real x 376..616) sits on
  top of the vanilla hotbar (real x 138..502), so the hotbar's right hand slots are covered by the
  bar and its selected slot. The bottom left health panel overlaps the hotbar's left end for the same
  reason at that width.

why this was not changed here
  the project has no safe area rule: the Little Bird's bottom right panel is anchored the same way
  (LittleBirdHud.java:90, width - panelWidth - 6) and overlaps the hotbar at narrow GUI widths too.
  Reserving space would mean inventing a design value, which the task rules forbid without your call.
