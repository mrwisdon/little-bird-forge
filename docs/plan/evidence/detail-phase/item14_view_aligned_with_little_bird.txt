alignment: the ground vehicle views now use the Little Bird's rules

differences that were aligned
  1. FOV keys - the ground hulls no longer have their own keys. GroundCamera reads exactly the two
     the Little Bird reads: third_person_fov_multiplier (exterior) and cockpit_fov_multiplier
     (turret interior). Deleted: ground_third_person_fov_multiplier, ground_interior_fov_multiplier.
  2. Interior eye height - positionRider now adds the shared cockpit_eye_lift_blocks (0.40) instead
     of a ground-only key. Deleted: ground_interior_camera_lift_blocks.
  3. Camera mode - GroundVehicleInput now mirrors LittleBirdInput.applyCameraMode: boarding saves
     the player's current camera and forces third person, the cockpit key switches to first person,
     and leaving restores the saved camera (a first person preference falls back to third person,
     which is what the aircraft do).
  4. Rider body - GroundRiderRender cancels the player render while riding, the same rule
     LittleBirdInput.onRenderPlayer already applies to the helicopter.

already consistent
  - the toggle key is the same cockpit key (C) the aircraft use.

deliberately NOT copied (ground hulls have no equivalent)
  - camera roll: the aircraft roll with the airframe's bank; a tank does not bank.
  - cockpit hidden model parts: the Little Bird hides its cabin/roof/rotor parts. The ground models
    do not need it - Minecraft culls box interior faces, so the turret walls are already see-through
    from inside, and the barrel must stay visible because it is what the fire control aims along.
  - shoulder view (right mouse over-the-shoulder): an aircraft-only convenience, not requested here.

evidence
  - item14_aligned_interior.png: interior camera via the shared cockpit key, fire control reading 18 m.
  - item14_aligned_camera_restored.png: after dismounting, the camera is back to third person and the
    player model is drawn again.
  - run/config/battlefield-client.toml now carries only the aircraft's three view keys:
        third_person_fov_multiplier = 1.35 / cockpit_fov_multiplier = 1.0 / cockpit_eye_lift_blocks = 0.4
