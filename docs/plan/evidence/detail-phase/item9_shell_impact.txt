defect found and fixed during this stage:
  the new main_gun_shell entity type had no renderer registered, so the client render
  dispatcher handed the frustum check a null renderer and the client crashed with a
  NullPointerException (run/crash-reports/crash-2026-09-12_19.06.40-client.txt).
  Fixed by adding client/MainGunShellRenderer (renders nothing on purpose: the fireball
  is the visual) and registering it in ClientModEvents. Re-ran the same steps: no new crash.
