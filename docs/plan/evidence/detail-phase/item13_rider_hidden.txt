stage 3: the crew's own body is no longer drawn on a ground hull

what changed
  - client/GroundRiderRender (new): RenderPlayerEvent.Pre is cancelled while the player rides a
    MainBattleTankEntity or an InfantryFightingVehicleEntity - the same rule the Apache already
    follows (ApacheRiderRender). Rendering only: the hull, turret, barrel, other units, the HUD and
    the entity hitbox (which comes from EntityType.sized) are untouched.

evidence
  - item13_rider_hidden_compare.png: left = before (the rider's head/back pokes out above the
    turret roof), right = after (no player model on the tank at all). Same tank, same camera.
  - item13_view_switch_stable.png: after three rounds of C (interior) / F5 (third person) switching
    the game is still up, the interior fire control still reads 18 m, no leftover player model, and
    no new crash report (run/crash-reports still holds only the two older files).
  - run/logs/latest.log errors are only the headless rig's own: the narrator cannot load libflite and
    OpenAL cannot open a device - both pre-date this work and are unrelated to the view system.

note: hiding is applied in both cameras (like the Apache) rather than only in the turret interior:
vanilla already hides the local body in first person, so an interior-only rule would have been a
no-op, while the third person body was visibly poking through the turret roof.
