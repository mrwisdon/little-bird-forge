fix: the tank's bottom-right readout was 12 GUI pixels wider than the weapon bar

symptom (measured on the pixels of align_exterior.png, a 854x480 shot)
  - readout cyan top edge : x 566..829  (264 px = 132 GUI)
  - weapon slot bar       : x 590..830  (240 px = 120 GUI)
  Right edges matched, the left edges differed by 24 px, so the readout stuck out to the left of
  the slots it belongs to.

cause
  GroundHudLayout had its own READOUT_WIDTH = 132 while the bar is barWidth(slots) = 120 for the
  tank's three slots, and both were positioned from the right margin. readout() also computed the
  bar's Y with the default three slot layout, so a two weapon hull would have placed the readout
  against the wrong bar height.

fix (layout only, no drawing changes)
  - deleted READOUT_WIDTH; the readout now takes the bar's own rect: same x, same width, and
    READOUT_HEIGHT above it, via readout(screenWidth, screenHeight, hudScale, rows).
  - GroundHud passes its real row count (TANK_ROWS / IFV_ROWS).
  - GroundHudLayoutTest: the readout must now match the bar on both edges, plus a new case pinning
    the two weapon hull's narrower bar and readout.

measured after the fix (854x480 shot)
  - readout cyan top edge : x 590..829  (240 px = 120 GUI)
  - whole cyan extent     : bbox 240x124 at offset +590,+314 -> readout, slots and legends share
    one left edge and one right edge.
